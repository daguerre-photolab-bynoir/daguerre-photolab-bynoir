02 — THE ORIGIN OF THE IMAGE

Who was Louis Daguerre?

Louis Daguerre was a French artist, theatrical designer, and one of photography’s defining pioneers. Working first with Nicéphore Niépce and later continuing their experiments, he developed the daguerreotype—a process that transformed light into a unique image on a silvered metal plate.

1826 Daguerre begins working with Nicéphore Niépce.
1839 The daguerreotype process is announced in Paris.
Today His devotion to light and process continues to inspire.

DAGUERRE takes inspiration from Daguerre’s belief that an image is created through both vision and process. It is not an attempt to reproduce the past—it is a continuation of its patience.
